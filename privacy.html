Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 26, 2026 • Version 1.0 • Applicable Worldwide

1. Overview & Scope

Tide Social ("we", "us", or "our") provides an interactive social media management, content planning, and AI creative studio workspace ("Service"). This Privacy Policy explains how personal data is collected, processed, and protected when you access our web application, mobile applications, or APIs.

Google Play & OAuth Compliance Guarantee: We do not sell your personal data. We do not use third-party advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. We only collect the minimal information necessary to deliver scheduling, analytics, and social publishing services at your direction.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Identifiers: Name, professional email address, workspace name, and profile handles when you register.
  • Connected Social Media Accounts: Public profile handles, account IDs, follower counts, and OAuth access tokens for platforms you explicitly connect (such as Instagram, LinkedIn, X, YouTube, TikTok, Facebook, Threads, Pinterest, Bluesky, Mastodon, Google Business, and Substack).
  • User-Generated Content: Post copy, drafts, scheduled dates, uploaded images, video assets, and idea bank concepts.
  • Operational Storage: We use browser localStorage to maintain offline drafts, responsive workspace state, and user preferences locally on your device.

3. How We Use Social Media OAuth Tokens

When you connect an account via OAuth 2.0:

  • Tokens are encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3 and stored with AES-256 encryption.
  • Tokens are used exclusively to execute user-initiated publishing, fetch public audience counts, and retrieve inbox comments.
  • We never collect or store your social platform passwords.
  • You may revoke OAuth access at any time through our in-app Channel Health settings or directly through the provider's security portal (e.g. Meta Account Center, Google Security Settings, LinkedIn Connected Apps).

4. Data Retention & Account Deletion

Your workspace data is retained only as long as your account remains active. In compliance with Google Play Store Developer Policy and global data protection regulations:

  • You may delete your data directly inside the app under Profile → Danger Zone.
  • You can also submit a deletion request without opening the application at our public portal: Account & Data Deletion Portal.
  • Upon receiving a deletion request, all personal identifiers, connected social tokens, and scheduled drafts are permanently purged within 30 days.

5. Children’s Privacy (COPPA)

Our Service is intended strictly for professional creators, businesses, and digital agencies. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain European jurisdictions). If we learn that such data has been collected, it is immediately deleted.

6. International Data Transfers & Security

Data is processed on secure cloud infrastructure hosted with enterprise-grade physical and electronic safeguards. All communication is enforced through HTTPS with strict transport security (HSTS).
